FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does the first meeting cost? +

The first consultation is complimentary and carries no obligation. It’s a chance for us to understand your situation and for you to decide whether we’re the right fit.

How are you paid? +

Fees are always disclosed upfront and in writing before any advice is implemented. No surprises.

Do I need a lot of money to get started? +

No. Good planning matters at every stage, whether you’re starting with R1,000 a month or managing a substantial portfolio.

Can we meet virtually? +

Absolutely. We meet in person or via video call — wherever you are in South Africa.

Will you work with my existing policies and investments? +

Yes. We start by reviewing what you already have and fine-tune from there.
