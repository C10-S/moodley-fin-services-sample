1. Commitment to POPIA Compliance

Moodley Financial respects your right to privacy and complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”). This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information submitted via our website (https://moodleyfinancial.co.za).

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry via our contact form or communicate with us directly, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address and telephone number
  • Details of your service enquiry or message

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your personal information solely to respond to your enquiries, prepare requested quotations, deliver contracted services, and fulfill legal or accounting obligations. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Security Safeguards

All website traffic is encrypted over HTTPS with strict Content Security Policy (CSP) headers. Contact enquiries are transmitted securely to our authorised workflow processor solely for customer support and quotation fulfilment.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction, updating, or deletion of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Withdraw consent or object to the processing of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Information Officer at hello@moodleyfinancial.co.za .
