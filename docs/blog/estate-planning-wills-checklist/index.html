Estate Planning & Wills: Protecting Your Family's Legacy in South Africa

Why an up-to-date Will, accurate estate liquidity calculations, and proper beneficiary nominations are essential to protect your loved ones.

Without a structured estate plan, executor fees, estate duty, capital gains tax, and administrative delays can erode what you have spent a lifetime building. Thoughtful estate planning ensures your assets pass smoothly, efficiently, and exactly as you intend.

Five Pillars of a Sound Estate Plan

  1. Will Drafting and Regular Review: Ensure your Will is valid, clear, and updated after major life events such as marriage, the birth of children, or buying property.
  2. Estate Liquidity and Duty Calculations: Calculate executor fees, master’s fees, liabilities, and taxes upfront so your family is never forced to sell assets to cover estate costs.
  3. Guardianship Planning for Minor Children: Nominate trusted guardians so your children are cared for according to your wishes.
  4. Trust Guidance: Understand when a testamentary or inter vivos trust is appropriate to protect minor beneficiaries or vulnerable dependants.
  5. Coordinated Beneficiary Nominations: Align life cover, retirement funds, and living annuities with your Will so payouts reach your beneficiaries promptly.
