Maximising Tax-Efficient Savings with TFSAs and Retirement Annuities

How South African earners and professionals can use every SARS allowance—from Tax-Free Savings Accounts to RAs—to grow wealth faster.

Paying less tax legally is one of the most reliable ways to accelerate long-term wealth creation. By combining the allowances provided by SARS with disciplined monthly contributions, your investment returns compound without unnecessary tax drag.

Key Allowances Every South African Investor Should Know

1. Tax-Free Savings Accounts (TFSAs)

Every South African individual can contribute up to the annual and lifetime limits into approved Tax-Free Savings Accounts. Inside a TFSA:

  • Zero tax on interest or dividends
  • Zero capital gains tax when you eventually withdraw
  • Ideal for long-term compounding across local and offshore equity unit trusts or ETFs

2. Retirement Fund Tax Deductions

Contributions toward a Retirement Annuity (RA), pension fund, or provident fund are tax-deductible up to 27.5% of your taxable income or remuneration (subject to the annual statutory cap). In addition, growth inside the fund is free of income tax, dividend withholding tax, and capital gains tax.

3. Capital Gains & Endowment Wrappers

For higher-income earners who have already maximised their annual interest exemptions and retirement deductions, endowments and structured unit trust portfolios help manage capital gains tax efficiently over time.
